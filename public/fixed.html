The calendar

Getting her real appointments into Sage.

I connected the apple calendars but nothing is coming through.

Two faults in the same place. Sage was reading Apple's reply in the wrong dialect, so it skipped every calendar — and underneath that, it was mangling every appointment's date, so they'd have been thrown away anyway.

Now: her iCloud calendar comes through, and a PT appointment still switches off her home PT rounds for that day.

bugfound by Regena

There was a button that popped up briefly that said refresh, but I can't find it now.

Not imagined. It was real, and it was sitting at the very bottom of a long screen where nobody would look.

Now: 🔄 Check for changes now is the first thing in that section.

bugfound by Regena

It shows to-do items which I don't want. For my calendar I just want appointments.

She was right on the principle, not just the preference — a calendar full of chores stops being a calendar.

Now: appointments only. There's a switch in Settings if she ever wants the to-dos there.

null ⚠️

That name came from Apple, not from Sage. iCloud marks a list that way when it won't share it properly with other apps.

Now: Settings explains whose mark it is, and there's a What did Apple send? button that shows exactly what her account handed over.

(found while chasing the one above)

Now: each one stands on its own and says why if it fails.

bug

iCloud will not share Reminders lists with any app outside Apple's own. Eleven lists on her phone, and iCloud offers none of them. That's Apple's boundary, not something Sage can work around.

Now: her Reminders app keeps doing its own job, and Sage has My lists instead.

Using it day to day

It just won't recognize my taps. If I close the app and go back in, it probably will work.

The worst one. Every time she moved between screens the app added another copy of the tap handler, so one tap fired twice and cancelled itself out — working or not working depending on whether the count was odd or even.

It was also quietly damaging her renewals: one tap on AARP moved it six years into the future.

Now: one tap, one action, every time.

bugfound by Regena

Sage asked a question and put the answer on screen as an unticked box — so of course she tried to type "yes". Worse, tapping save with nothing ticked would have thrown away everything she'd written.

Now: the question says "you answer by ticking below", the button says what it will do, and nothing gets binned.

Routines

I'm not sure how to edit a routine. I wanted to add a lake seasonal open and seasonal close.

She couldn't. The ability to make and change routines had been in the app since day one with no way to reach it.

Now: More → 📋 Routines → ＋ New routine, or Change this routine on any of them. For the lake, choose Certain months of the year and tap April to open, October to close.

newfound by Regena

I can't actually see the routine unless the event opens.

Now: the Routines screen lists every routine with all its steps, whether or not today is its day — and today's can be ticked off right there.
